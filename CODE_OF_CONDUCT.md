# ContactCore Code of Conduct

ContactCore is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free, privacy-respecting open-source community. Technical disagreement is expected; disrespect, intimidation, discrimination, harassment, threats, or publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- being respectful and constructive in reviews/issues/discussions;
- critiquing code/design rather than people;
- explaining reasoning and tradeoffs without belittling questions;
- welcoming contributors with different experience levels;
- accepting good-faith correction;
- protecting personal information shared accidentally;
- using fictional data when discussing ContactCore contact/database bugs publicly;
- focusing on reproducible facts when resolving disputes.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discriminatory remarks, slurs, or personal attacks;
- threats or encouragement of harm;
- repeated hostile/dismissive behavior after being asked to stop;
- sexualized or otherwise inappropriate content/attention;
- doxxing or publishing private contact/account/location information without permission;
- intentionally exposing another person's ContactCore database/export/backup;
- impersonation or deceptive attribution;
- sustained disruption of repository collaboration;
- retaliation against someone who reports a conduct/security concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in project-controlled spaces such as:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and repository interactions;
- official project communication/support channels;
- community spaces/events where someone is representing ContactCore/project maintainers.

## Privacy in community interactions

Because ContactCore manages contact data, community conduct includes respecting data privacy.

If someone accidentally posts personal information:

- do not quote/repost/spread it;
- notify maintainers privately when possible;
- help remove/redact the material;
- use fictional reproduction data instead.

For undisclosed security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` rather than posting exploit details publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, hide, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Depending on severity/repetition, maintainers may warn participants, restrict interaction, temporarily block participation, or permanently ban participation from project spaces where platform controls allow.

Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and focused on protecting the community rather than escalating conflict.

## Reporting a conduct concern

Report conduct concerns privately to:

- **sanskarin@outlook.in**

Include only the information necessary to understand the incident. Avoid forwarding unrelated private information.

For security vulnerabilities instead use the security-reporting process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are expected to:

- apply standards consistently regardless of contributor status;
- avoid using moderation to silence ordinary technical disagreement;
- protect reporter privacy as reasonably possible;
- recuse themselves when personally conflicted if another maintainer is available;
- distinguish conduct problems from normal code-quality/review decisions.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open-source community-conduct principles, including the Contributor Covenant, but is written specifically for ContactCore's repository/community needs.
